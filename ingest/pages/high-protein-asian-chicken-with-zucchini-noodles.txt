High-protein Asian chicken with zucchini noodles

These Asian Chicken Zoodles are a quick, 25-minute dinner made with lean chicken, fresh vegetables, and zucchini noodles for a light, low-carb twist on classic stir-fry.

Lean chicken breast provides high-quality protein that supports muscle repair and lean body mass. Its low-fat, high-protein profile helps promote fullness, making it a helpful choice for weight management and balanced eating. Chicken is also rich in B vitamins, which support energy production.

Mushrooms add savory depth while contributing important nutrients. They are the only produce that naturally contains vitamin D and also help support a healthy gut microbiome.

Zucchini noodles keep the dish light and vegetable-forward. Zucchini is low in calories and rich in fiber, which supports digestion, heart health, and blood glucose regulation.

Thanks to sesame oil, ginger, and garlic, this Asian Chicken Zoodles recipe delivers bold flavor in a healthy, low-carb meal you can enjoy any day of the week.

Asian Chicken Zoodles

Ingredients:

1 tablespoon sesame oil

2 cloves garlic, minced and divided

15 oz chicken breast, diced into bite-sized pieces

1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil

¾ cup chopped white and green onions, chopped

½ teaspoon minced fresh ginger, minced

1 cup sliced ​​mushrooms

2 teaspoons low-sodium soy sauce

1 teaspoon sesame seeds

4 cups spiralized zucchini

Preparation:

Marinate the chicken: In a medium bowl, combine half of the minced garlic with the sesame oil. Add the diced chicken and toss to coat evenly. Set aside.
Sauté the vegetables: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onions, remaining garlic, and ginger. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until fragrant and softened. Add the mushrooms and cook for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender. Transfer the vegetables to a plate.
Cook the chicken: In the same skillet, cook the marinated chicken for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until fully cooked and lightly golden.
Combine and season: Return the vegetables to the skillet. Stir in the soy sauce and cook for 1 to 2 minutes.
Add the zucchini noodles: Add the spiralized zucchini and cook for 30 to 60 seconds, just until slightly tender.
Serve: Remove from heat and sprinkle with sesame seeds before serving.

Pro Tip

How to Prevent Watery Zoodles: Zucchini releases moisture quickly when heated. To keep your dish from becoming watery, avoid overcooking and sauté the zoodles briefly over medium heat. For extra moisture control, lightly pat spiralized zucchini with paper towels before cooking.
