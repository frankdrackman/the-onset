Comfort food is often associated with heavier dishes, but this recipe shows how familiar favorites can evolve to meet today’s wellness goals. This Creamy Chicken and Mushroom recipe reimagines the classic feel of chicken stroganoff by keeping the creamy experience people love and pairing it with ingredients that nourish the body. Everything cooks in one pot, making it a practical and versatile option for any time you want something warm, satisfying, and healthy.

Mushrooms that support gut and immune health

Mushrooms add rich flavor and a hearty texture while offering meaningful health benefits. They are naturally low in fat and calories, which supports weight management, and they act as prebiotics that help feed healthy gut bacteria.

Ricotta cheese for creaminess and strength

Instead of traditional heavy cream, this recipe uses a small amount of skim ricotta cheese to create a smooth, velvety sauce. Ricotta is rich in calcium and phosphorus, two minerals that support strong bones. It also provides high-quality protein that helps with fullness and steady energy.

Creamy Chicken Stroganoff with Mushrooms – Easy One-Pot Recipe

Ingredients:

8 oz chicken breast, sliced into small strips

2 cloves garlic, minced

1 tbsp olive oil

½ cup onion, diced

¼ cup bell pepper, diced

¼ cup skim ricotta cheese

2 tsp low sodium soy sauce or coconut aminos**

1 cup sliced mushrooms

3 tbsp fresh Italian (long leaf) parsley, chopped

** Coconut aminos is a soy sauce alternative made from the fermented sap of coconut blossoms. It does not taste like coconut and has a mild, slightly sweet, savory flavor similar to soy sauce but with significantly less sodium. It is naturally gluten free and soy free, making it a good option for people following low-sodium or allergy-friendly diets. You can find it in most grocery stores near the soy sauce or in the gluten-free section.

Preparation:

1. Season the chicken: In a medium bowl, combine the sliced chicken, minced garlic, a pinch of salt, and a pinch of pepper. Set aside.

2. Sauté the vegetables: Heat the olive oil in a large nonstick skillet or wide saucepan over medium heat. Add the diced onion, diced bell pepper, and sliced mushrooms. Sauté for about 5 minutes or until the vegetables soften.

3. Cook the chicken: Add the seasoned chicken to the pan. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken begins to turn golden.

4. Build the sauce: Stir in the low sodium soy sauce or coconut aminos, and the ricotta cheese. Mix well to combine.

5. Simmer until creamy: Reduce heat to low and cook for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.

6. Finish and serve: Remove from heat and sprinkle with fresh Italian parsley before serving.

Serves 2

Total preparation time 30 minutes
