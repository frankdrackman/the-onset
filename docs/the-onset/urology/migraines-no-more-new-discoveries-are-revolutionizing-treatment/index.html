Migraines no more? New discoveries are revolutionizing treatment.

By Cynthia E. Armand, MD, Attending Physician, Neurology · 3 min read · ¿Adiós a las migrañas? Llegan tratamientos revolucionarios

In short

Neuroscientists have recently made some important discoveries about what causes migraines, including that migraine sufferers have higher levels of CGRP, a protein in the brain and nervous system involved in the transmission of pain. These discoveries have led to the development of innovative medical treatments and non-pharmaceutical therapies. As a result, more personalized, targeted holistic treatment plans are the new approach for treating — and even preventing — migraines, with breakthrough improvements in health, wellness, and quality of life for those who suffer from them.

Migraine prevention is now more possible than ever

The latest medical treatments are a game-changer

The American Migraine Foundation calls CGRP inhibitors “the biggest news in migraine treatment and prevention in decades.” Since we now know that people who have migraines have higher levels of CGRP in the blood, this new class of super medications targets this protein, interrupting the brain processes that lead to episodes. They are available in pill, injectable, and nasal-spray form and are the very first medications developed specifically for preventing both episodic (up to 14 headaches a month) and chronic (15 or more) migraines, with or without visual auras. While CGRP is not the culprit of all migraines, these therapies are considered “miracle drugs” for some, who can go from 15 or more debilitating chronic migraines per month to just one — or even none. Another therapeutic approach proven to be effective in preventing migraines is the combination of these and other medications, including treating people with Botox and certain antidepressants called tricyclics to improve care.

Complementary therapies are also game-changing

Complementary therapies used for migraine maintenance, including vitamin regimens and behavioral changes, are now proven to control migraine triggers. Magnesium, riboflavin, vitamin B2, and coenzyme Q10 are evidence-based, having proven effective in warding off migraine symptoms. And behavioral therapies like mindfulness meditation, stress-reduction practices, and cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) are now proven to control migraine triggers. In addition, biofeedback, a mind–body technique that involves monitoring physical responses to stress, has been shown to help reduce the frequency and severity of migraines.

Everyone’s brain and body physiology are different, so what might work for one person may not work for another, and family history is key in identifying the most effective combination of therapies for you. Your primary care doctor is your first stop, and they can determine if they should refer you to a neurologist, who can provide basic treatment for episodic migraines. If these basic treatments are ineffective, your neurologist may refer you to a migraine specialist for a more personalized, targeted treatment plan using newer, more sophisticated therapies optimized just for you.

Today, there is no reason to suffer from migraines when we have so many treatments and therapies available, a combination of which has the potential to greatly improve quality of life.

Montefiore Einstein is at the forefront of headache disorder treatment and research

The Montefiore Einstein Headache Center is the first center of its kind in the world and an international leader in the field focused exclusively on the diagnosis and treatment of headache disorders and facial pain in adults and children. Our center has more than 70 years of experience advancing clinical excellence and knowledge of headache disorders, while our research has informed the development of the classification system for migraine and other headache syndromes, leading to their precision treatments.

Our interdisciplinary team of renowned experts treats the full spectrum of pediatric and adult headache disorders — including migraine, tension-type headache, hypnic headache, and cluster headache, as well as those secondary to other medical and neurological conditions — to help minimize pain, optimize function, and improve quality of life.

We’re ranked in the top 1% of all U.S. hospitals for neurology and neurosurgery, and Montefiore Einstein’s Burke Rehabilitation Hospital is also ranked in the top 1% of all U.S. hospitals for rehabilitation, according to U.S. News & World Report.
