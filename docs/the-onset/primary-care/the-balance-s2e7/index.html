In short

Managing pain with virtual reality. A new frontier. Don’t miss S2 of The Balance with Dr. Philip O. Ozuah, President & CEO, Montefiore Einstein. Dr. Ozuah’s guests are Montefiore Einstein Internal Medicine Specialist, Dr. Hector Perez, and Irene Cruz and Daran Porter, who by changing what their brains perceived, changed their experience of pain.
