In short

Eight glasses a day? See what can happen when we don’t get enough water. Don’t miss S2 of The Balance with Dr. Philip O. Ozuah, President & CEO, Montefiore Einstein. Dr. Ozuah’s guests are Montefiore Einstein Internal Medicine Specialist, Dr. Amanda Dela Cruz, and Carl LaRiviere, amateur basketball player, who learned the hard way that he wasn’t drinking as much water as he was losing.
