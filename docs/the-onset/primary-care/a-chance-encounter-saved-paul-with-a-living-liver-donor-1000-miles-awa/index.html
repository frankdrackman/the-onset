A chance encounter saved Paul with a living liver donor 1000 miles away

By Milan Kinkhabwala, MD, Chief of Transplantation Surgery, Director of Abdominal Transplantation, and Director of the Hepatobiliary Cancer Program · 3 min read

In short

When the New York–based Gibson, a 45-year-old British scientist, first met 39-year-old Jeremy Jones, the director of a small business in Memphis, Tennessee, he learned that Jeremy dons superhero costumes to visit sick children in the hospital in his spare time. So while Paul knew that Jeremy was a unique character, he had no idea that the man would one day save his life.

They say not all heroes wear capes, but Paul Gibson found one who actually does.

The two had met just a couple of times in Tennessee, first at a wedding and later when a mutual friend took Paul to a Halloween party at Jeremy’s house in Memphis. Paul had moved to New York from Tennessee for his wife Tanya’s job. The move to the city was a welcome change, but Paul began to have some health concerns soon after. His arms started getting thin, and he noticed red spots on his skin.

Blood tests indicated serious liver problems, so Paul’s doctor referred him to Montefiore Einstein. By the time we saw him, his liver was failing, causing life-threatening health complications. Paul would need a new liver, but there was little hope he would reach the top of the transplant list in time.

But even in the high-tech world of modern medicine, luck plays a huge role.

Paul’s company had produced a two-minute video to help him find an altruistic “living donor” who could help save his life. When Jeremy’s wife showed him their acquaintance Paul’s video on Facebook, Jeremy felt compelled to act — even more so because he was a match with Paul’s blood type.

On the day of the surgery, a segment of Jeremy’s liver was removed, and then our team quickly transplanted it to Paul. Both operations were successful. Now, a few years after their operations, both men have fully recovered. Jeremy is back working in Memphis and has convened an entire troupe of superheroes to visit hospitals and bring smiles to the faces of sick children. Paul has a new lease on life and is appreciating the quality time he can now spend with Tanya, with whom he travels while visiting family around the world.

As transplant surgeons, we always form a special connection with our patients, but it’s especially heartwarming to see the strong bond that these two men and their families now share.

To save additional lives, we need more superheroes like Jeremy to come forward. A living organ donation is a faster way to a lifesaving transplant, and each living donor actually helps more than one person. Every time a patient like Paul is taken off the transplant list, another sick person moves up, which gives them a better chance of finding a lifesaving donor.

Montefiore Einstein: A national leader in transplant and among the highest-ranked in live organ donation

Montefiore Einstein has more than 60 years of experience in adult and pediatric transplants, and our breakthroughs with minimally invasive laparoscopic techniques have made living donor surgeries safer, simpler, and easier on our patients. Our adult and pediatric kidney-transplant survival rate is the highest in New York State, with 100% pediatric kidney transplant survival, and our liver-transplant survival rate is among the best in the United States, with 100% survival for live-donor liver transplants. Montefiore Einstein also has one of the nation’s longest-running kidney-transplant programs, as well as outstanding programs in heart, lung, liver, and pancreas transplantation.

We encourage you to take a moment to see the powerful, moving stories that some of our patients have created using the new tool on our I Want to Live website. If you would like to become a living donor or perhaps need a lifesaving transplant, please visit our I Want to Live website or call Living Organ Donor–Liver at 1-888-RXLIVER (1-888-795-4837) or Living Organ Donor–Kidney at 1-877-CUREKDNY (1-877-287-3536) to speak with one of our transplant associate specialists.
