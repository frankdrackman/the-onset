Celebrate Father's Day with one of these tasty and healthy dinner

Reviewed by Sandra Arevalo, RDN, Primary Care · 2 min read

In short

A delicious, healthy dinner al fresco is the perfect way to show Dad your appreciation. Invite relatives, friends and neighbors, or keep the celebration to just your immediate family.

This Father’s Day, celebrate with a festive cookout or family-style meal that’s sure to put a smile on Dad’s face.

The first recipe featured below, BBQ Chicken and Sauce, is a healthy take on a summer classic that’s perfect for a barbeque or picnic in the park. BBQ sauce is typically high in sugar and fat, but this recipe only uses a small amount of brown sugar. We’re also using boneless chicken breasts to reduce the amount of saturated fat while still providing a good source of protein.

Simple and flavorful ingredients like Dijon mustard, cayenne and ground chili pepper make this dish healthy and delicious. Serve it with a big salad and a bowl of fresh fruit for a well-rounded meal.

If you prefer to celebrate Father’s Day with a sit-down dinner, the Vegetable Lasagna is a favorite among children and adults alike. Packed with vegetables, this dish is high in vitamin C and iron. The more vegetables you include, the tastier the lasagna will be. Simply slice up some veggies –– such as broccoli, eggplant and zucchini –– and add them to the dish.

Using three different cheeses makes this lasagna a great source of protein. Plus, by using part-skim mozzarella and ricotta cheeses, we’re reducing the amount of fat and adding an extra boost of calcium. High-protein, low-fat and rich in carbohydrates –– this dish is a nutritional powerhouse. Consider using whole wheat lasagna noodles to increase the amount of dietary fiber.

Both the BBQ Chicken and Sauce and Vegetable Lasagna recipes were featured in the cookbook titled “Microwave Chef” and can be made with nothing more than a microwave, a few healthy ingredients and basic cooking skills. The cookbook began in 2002 and grew out of Montefiore’s shared collaboration with the Children’s Health Fund to help families who were experiencing housing instability learn how to prepare healthy, affordable and tasty foods without access to a full kitchen.

Interested in learning about Montefiore’s commitment to make cooking fun and create a healthy community? Visit www.montefiore.org.
