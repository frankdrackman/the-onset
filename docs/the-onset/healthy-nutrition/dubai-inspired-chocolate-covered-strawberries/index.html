Dubai inspired chocolate covered strawberries

Reviewed by Monica Kelly, MS, Healthy Nutrition · 3 min read · Fresas con Chocolate al Estilo Dubái

In short

Dubai style chocolate has become widely popular for its rich flavors and signature ingredients such as pistachios and dark chocolate. This recipe draws inspiration from that trend while offering an option that supports healthier everyday choices, using fresh fruit and carefully chosen ingredients.

  • Reviewed by Monica Kelly, MS, Montefiore Einstein

Dark chocolate with at least seventy percent cocoa forms the base of this dessert. It brings a deep, satisfying flavor along with antioxidants and flavanols that support heart and brain health. Compared to milk chocolate, dark chocolate naturally contains less sugar, making it a more thoughtful choice when enjoying something sweet.

Fresh strawberries add brightness, color, and a refreshing contrast to the richer flavors of the other ingredients. Naturally low in calories and rich in antioxidants, they provide vitamin C, a nutrient that supports immune health and plays an important role in tissue repair and wound healing.

Pistachios, used both whole and as natural pistachio cream, add texture and richness. They are a plant based source of protein and heart healthy unsaturated fats, and they are naturally high in fiber. These nutrients support digestion, help promote satiety, and contribute to steadier blood sugar levels.

Dark chocolate, strawberries, and pistachios come together in a way that highlights simple preparation and ingredient quality. The result is a lighter way to experience the textures and tastes that have made Dubai style chocolate so trendy.

Dubai-Style Chocolate Covered Strawberries

Ingredients:

3.5 ounces dark chocolate, at least 70 percent cocoa

2 tablespoons of unsweetened almond milk

2 cups whole strawberries, washed and completely dried

⅓ cup natural pistachio cream, made from 100 percent pistachios with no added sugar**

2 tablespoons crushed pistachios

**NOTE: If natural pistachio cream is not available, you can make a simple version at home. Place 1 cup unsalted pistachios with shells removed in a food processor with ¾ cup powdered sugar and blend until a thick paste forms. In a small saucepan over low heat (or in the microwave), combine the ½ cup white chocolate, 2 tbsp butter, and ½ cup almond milk. Stir continuously until completely melted and smooth. Add the white chocolate mixture to the pistachio paste in the food processor and blend until smooth. If the cream is too thick, add more milk. Store any extra pistachio cream in an airtight container in the refrigerator.

Preparation:

Melt the dark chocolate: Place the dark chocolate in a heat-safe bowl. Melt it using a double boiler over low heat, or microwave in 20 second intervals, stirring after each interval until fully melted and smooth. Add the almond milk: Stir the almond milk into the melted chocolate until the mixture becomes silky, smooth, and glossy. Set aside. Prepare the strawberries: Arrange the strawberries on a serving plate or a parchment-lined tray. Using a small spoon or spatula, gently spread a layer of pistachio cream over each strawberry, coating the top evenly. Finish with chocolate and pistachios: Spoon or drizzle the melted dark chocolate over the pistachio cream covered strawberries, letting it flow naturally. Sprinkle the crushed pistachios on top while the dark chocolate is still warm. Chill until set: Place the strawberries in the refrigerator for 10 to 15 minutes, or until the chocolate is fully set. Serve and enjoy: Once set, the strawberries are ready to serve.

Yield: 8 to 10 servings, one strawberry per serving

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Servings: 8-10
