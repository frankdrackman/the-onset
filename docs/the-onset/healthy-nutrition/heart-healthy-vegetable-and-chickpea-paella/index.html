Heart-healthy vegetable and chickpea paella

Reviewed by Monica Kelly, MS, Healthy Nutrition · 2 min read · Paella cardiosaludable de garbanzos y vegetales

In short

This vegetable and chickpea paella is a delightful vegetarian take on the traditional Spanish rice dish. It’s one of my go-to dinners when I want something healthy, high in fiber, and quick to prepare.

  • Reviewed by Monica Kelly, MS, Montefiore Einstein

Enjoy this Spanish classic all year round.

Chickpeas are among my favorite legumes for both their health benefits and their savory taste. Their low glycemic index helps regulate blood sugar, and they’re an excellent source of plant-based protein for vegetarians, vegans, or anyone trying to follow a plant-based diet. They’re also rich in choline, which helps support brain and nervous system function. This recipe packs in even more protein by adding kidney beans. Not only are they also a great source of dietary fiber, they contain vitamin B1, which supports cognitive function.

Brown long-grain rice helps you feel satiated for longer thus aiding in weight management. Because it contains both the bran and germ of the rice, it also adds an extra boost of dietary fiber when compared with white rice. The addition of diced zucchini, another food with a low glycemic index, contributes the antioxidants lutein and zeaxanthin, which are important for our vision.

I hope you enjoy this colorful paella dish as much as I do — it’s perfect for summer evenings and can be on your dinner table in under an hour.

Red onion, however, is the secret star of this recipe. It’s rich in antioxidants, which combat the damage caused by free radicals in the body. Additionally, the sulfur compounds in red onions support heart health, lowering inflammation and promoting healthy circulation throughout the body. It also provides prebiotics, which help maintain a robust balance of gut bacteria and support digestion.

½ cup of cooked or canned kidney beans, rinsed and drained

1 cup cooked or canned chickpeas, rinsed and drained
