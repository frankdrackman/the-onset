Experience the Mediterranean with this Greek Salad with Sardines

Reviewed by Jessica Shapiro, MS, Montefiore · 3 min read

In short

Welcome an escape from your regular meal preparation routine by experiencing a bit of the Mediterranean. According to the American Heart Association, “year after year, the Mediterranean diet comes out in the U.S. News and World Report annual ranking of best diets.” The Mediterranean-style diet is primarily plant-based with a strong foundation consisting of vegetables, fruits, whole grains, nuts, and olive oil as the primary fat source. Complementing the foundation of plant-based foods are moderate amounts of dairy, eggs, fish, and poultry; while limiting the less desirable ingredients such as added sugar, simple carbohydrates, and saturated fats. It does the heart and body good! So, try this tasty salad with traditional Mediterranean flare by including ingredients rich in nutrients such as sardines, chickpeas, and low-fat cheese. Try this tasty salad filled with healthy omega-3 enriched sardines

Oooh!…the Mediterranean. In addition to conjuring up images of the beautiful scenery, rich culture, and people from the countries that border the Mediterranean Sea, you also think of the food. Mama Mia! This Greek salad with sardines embodies the key features of a Mediterranean diet with plenty of plant-based ingredients, extra virgin olive oil, a splash of fish, and a dash of feta cheese. So, go ahead… experience the Mediterranean!

While they may not be as popular in American cuisines, sardines are a staple in the diet of countries within the Mediterranean region and others like the Philippines and Portugal. These small fish are soft, scale-free, oily, meaty, and full of flavor. Whether packaged or fresh, sardines can be enjoyed straight out of the can or quickly prepared on the grill. Sardines are delicious with just a splash of lemon, topped onto a cracker, paired with avocado, or added as a topping on a salad, pizza, or your favorite dish. Because of their versatility, low mercury levels, and sustainability, they can be enjoyed by the whole family, even pregnant women. So, even if unfamiliar, you need not hesitate to taste sardines!

Any fish with an average mercury concentration less than or equal to 0.15 µg/g was placed in the ‘best choices – eat 2-3 servings a week’ category.

Don’t like sardines? No worries! You can substitute them with water-based canned tuna as a tasty alternative. But make sure to check the mercury levels on the can of all fish products to ensure that they are within safe limits. According to the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), “Any fish with an average mercury concentration less than or equal to 0.15 µg/g was placed in the ‘best choices – eat 2-3 servings a week’ category.” Consuming high levels of mercury runs the risk of toxicity which can lead to health problems such as cognitive disorders, so you want to adhere to the recommended consumption levels.

Plant-based diets are nutrient-dense, rich in vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and more vital to good health. Almost as necessary for your health is limiting foods and ingredients that can be unhealthful such as simple carbohydrates, excessive added sugars, animal-based saturated fats, and sodium. This Greek salad with sardines is full of nuggets of nutrition that will do your heart and body good.

Rich in omega-3 fatty acids, sardines provide a buffet of health benefits as it is an excellent source of calcium and vitamin D, which promote bone health and may reduce the risk of heart disease and support cognitive health. Eating sardines has been attributed to alleviating ailments such as depression, inflammation, and heart disease. Consider incorporating this fish as a healthy dietary option into your regular meal plan.

Remember, next time you prepare a meal, experience the Mediterranean by adding some sardines. They are a great inclusion that will yield nuggets of nutrition. This Greek salad with sardines is a great place to start!
