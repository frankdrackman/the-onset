Common questions

Do I need a probiotic supplement?

For most healthy adults, no. Fermented foods and a high-fibre diet do the same work more cheaply and with better evidence behind them. Probiotics have specific, studied uses — after certain antibiotics, for example — and those are worth discussing with a clinician rather than guessing at in a shop.

How much fibre should I be eating?

Most adults need roughly 25 to 30 grams a day and most get about half that. Increase it gradually and with water, or the first week will convince you it was a bad idea.
