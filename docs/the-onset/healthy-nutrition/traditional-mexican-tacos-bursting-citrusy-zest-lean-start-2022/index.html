Traditional Mexican tacos bursting with citrusy zest for a lean start to

By Jessica Shapiro, MS, CDCES, for Montefiore · 2 min read

In short

Mexican food is known for its colorful ingredients bursting with spices that create a pleasurable explosion of textures and flavors in your mouth. The taco is a classic Mexican dish consisting of a small tortilla that is wrapped around a filling and simply garnished. The taco’s ease of preparation, adaptability of ingredients, and reliability for creating tasty concoctions is now ubiquitous in cultures and cuisines all over the world. See for yourself by whipping up this delicious and nutritious fish taco recipe. You will delight in the tanginess of the grapefruit* and the richness of the texture of codfish, accompanied by ingredients and herbs traditionally found in Mexican-inspired menus. Bon Apetite! ¡Buen provecho!

As you dive into this dish, you can savor the burst of interwoven flavors while knowing that you are enjoying a meal that is a great source of vitamins, minerals, and healthy fats for your body. Codfish is one of the finest sources of omega-3 fats, B vitamins such as B12, and fat-soluble vitamins like A, E and D, helping to support your nervous system and blood cells, boost your energy levels, improve brain function and reduce your risk for heart disease. When you complement codfish with grapefruit, it becomes fortified with loads of Vitamin C, Vitamin A, fiber, and the all-powerful lycopene, providing additional health benefits such as facilitating digestion and supporting immune health. Further, avocado, lime, and cabbage function as supporting ingredients that aid gut health and are a fantastic source of antioxidants that also have anti-inflammatory properties. All in all, this recipe packs a punch on the nutritional side and fuels your body with a straightforward yet scrumptious recipe that you can even eat with one hand.

So, if “healthy eating” made the list of your 2022 resolutions, jumpstart the New Year by including these Fish Tacos with Grapefruit Dressing recipe in your meal plan. Cheers to a Healthier You!

*Of note: Some drugs become more potent when combined with grapefruit, and those on statins should avoid it. Consult your doctor if taking any medications before eating grapefruit.

*The % Daily Value (DV) tells you how much a nutrient in a food serving contributes to a daily diet. 2,000 calories a day is used for general nutrition advice.
