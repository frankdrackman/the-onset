Cozy fall soup with pumpkin, sweet potato, lentils & turmeric

Reviewed by Monica Kelly, MS, Healthy Nutrition · 2 min read · Sopa de otoño reconfortante con calabaza, batata y lentejas

In short

A bowl of soup can be more than comfort; it can also be a powerful way to nourish your body. This pumpkin soup with sweet potato, lentils, and turmeric blends seasonal flavor with proven health benefits, making it a recipe that supports both strength and well-being.

  • Reviewed by Monica Kelly, MS, Montefiore Einstein

Seasonal comfort in a bowl

Sweet Potato: Antioxidant Power and Blood Sugar Support

Sweet potatoes add natural sweetness and a vibrant boost of antioxidants. Their deep orange color comes from beta-carotene, which protects cells and may reduce the risk of chronic disease. Packed with vitamin A and vitamin C for immune support, sweet potatoes also deliver plenty of fiber to aid digestion. Thanks to their low glycemic index and high fiber content, sweet potatoes also support digestion and blood sugar management.

Celebrated in Ayurvedic and traditional Chinese medicine, turmeric provides natural anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits that support healing and overall wellness.

Pumpkin contributes creaminess and depth while remaining light. Low in calories and high in water, pumpkin helps you feel satisfied without being heavy. It’s rich in vitamins A, C, and E, as well as antioxidants that strengthen the immune system and help the body fight infections, an especially valuable benefit during colder months.

Lentils bring hearty texture and plant-based protein, making this soup filling enough for a full meal. They’re packed with fiber for digestive support and rich in B vitamins, including folate and iron. These nutrients are essential for blood health, heart function, and long-lasting energy.

Turmeric ties the soup together with its golden color and warm flavor. Celebrated in Ayurvedic and traditional Chinese medicine, turmeric provides natural anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits that support healing and overall wellness.

Creamy, nutrient-dense, and satisfying, this healthy fall soup is a simple, versatile recipe to enjoy for lunch, dinner, or make in batches to enjoy several days.

1 ½ cups cubed pumpkin (or a winter squash such as butternut, acorn, or kabocha)

1 ½ cups cubed sweet potato with skins removed

2 cups cooked lentils, drained or one 15 oz can, drained and rinsed
