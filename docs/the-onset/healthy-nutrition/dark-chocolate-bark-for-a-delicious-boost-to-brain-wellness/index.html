Dark chocolate bark for a delicious boost to brain wellness

Reviewed by Monica Kelly, MS, Healthy Nutrition · 2 min read · Corteza de chocolate oscuro, un dulce placer que beneficia la salud del cerebro

In short

Celebrate the holidays with a dark chocolate bark that is decadent and nourishing at the same time. Made with antioxidant-rich dark chocolate, fresh berries, and crunchy nuts, this simple recipe comes together in minutes and delivers both flavor and wellness in every bite. It’s an easy way to enjoy something indulgent while still feeling good about what’s on your plate.

  • Reviewed by Monica Kelly, MS, Montefiore Einstein

Dark Chocolate for Heart and Brain Health

Start with dark chocolate chips or chunks that are at least 70 percent cocoa dark chocolate. This variety is naturally rich in antioxidants such as flavonoids and polyphenols, which help the body combat oxidative stress. Dark chocolate has been linked to improved heart health and cognitive function, offering a delicious way to support both mind and body.

Bright, Flavorful Berries

Adding fresh mixed berries gives this dessert a burst of color and freshness. Berries are packed with antioxidants that protect cells, reduce inflammation, and help the body fight infections. They are also known to support heart and brain health, and they are the perfect balance to the richness of dark chocolate.

Crunchy, Nutrient-Rich Nuts

Finish your bark with chopped hazelnuts or almonds for a satisfying crunch. Both nuts contain heart-healthy monounsaturated fats along with vitamin E, magnesium, and fiber. These nutrients help support cardiovascular health, digestion, and immune function while adding texture and depth to each bite.

Simple to Make, Hard to Resist

With just three ingredients this treat comes together in minutes. It is easy to make, beautiful to serve, and full of natural goodness.

Dark Chocolate Bark with Berries

Ingredients:

1 cup dark chocolate chips or chunks (at least 70% cocoa)

1 cup mixed fresh berries, chopped

¼ cup hazelnuts or almonds, chopped

Preparation:

Prepare the baking sheet line a rectangular baking sheet with parchment paper. Melt the chocolate, place the dark chocolate in a heat-safe bowl set over a pot of simmering water (double boiler) and gently stir at regular intervals or microwave in 20-second intervals, stirring after each, until smooth and fully melted. Assemble the bark, pour the melted chocolate onto the parchment paper and spread it into a thin rectangle with a spatula. Sprinkle the chopped berries and nuts evenly over the top. Gently press them into the chocolate. Chill until firm place the baking sheet in the refrigerator for about 1 hour, or until the chocolate hardens completely. Serve and store once set, break or cut the bark into 2-inch pieces. Enjoy immediately or store leftovers in the freezer to enjoy later.

Makes 20

Total prep time 10 minutes (unrefrigerated)
