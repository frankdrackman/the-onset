Simple, healthy, and full of Middle Eastern flavor: Lentil Falafel with yogurt sauce

By Montefiore, Healthy Nutrition · 2 min read

In short

If you're a fan of Middle Eastern cuisine, you're probably no stranger to the savory delights of falafel. Its delightful blend of aromatic herbs and spices have made it a street-food staple in the Middle East, and nowadays, you can find it all over the US as a meat alternative. And while the original recipe is certainly no slouch when it comes to nutrition, I realized there are a couple of simple ways to transform the traditional recipe using different ingredients, while keeping the essence intact.

Traditionally, falafel is made with chickpeas or fava beans and a blend of herbs and spices. Instead, we're turning to lentils as the star ingredient. Health-conscious eaters tend to love chickpeas, but lentils don’t need to live in its shadows. For starters, they bring to the table more fiber and protein, as well as larger doses of iron, vitamins B6 and B9, and phosphorus (an essential nutrient for the upkeep of all the tissues and cells in your body). The other twist here is we’ll be oven-baking our falafel instead of frying; A perfect cooking method that requires way less oil. You can also try using an air fryer instead.

The co-star of this dish is the tangy yogurt sauce. Yogurt is not only a decent source of calcium and protein, but it also delivers good probiotics for optimal gut health. Feel free to use any vegan yogurt of your choice. The sauce itself is super easy to make ahead of time and refrigerate, so it's ready to go when you are. It can be put aside and saved for other dishes or used as a healthier alternative to creamy salad dressing.
