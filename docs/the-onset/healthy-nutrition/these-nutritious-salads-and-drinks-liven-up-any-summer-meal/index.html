These nutritious salads and drinks will liven up any summer meal

By Jessica Shapiro, MS, CDCES, for Montefiore · 3 min read · Estas ensaladas y bebidas nutritivas realzan cualquier comida de verano

In short

Salads are often appreciated on hot summer days, but many may still view them as bland or boring, and something a doctor encourages them to eat rather than something they can look forward to enjoying. That doesn’t need to be the case at all. With a little creativity, salads can become a party of flavors that will make it to your list of favorite recipes. With warm weather upon us, here are two of my favorite salad recipes, along with a few flavorful drink options to enjoy on a hot summer day.

The first dish — watermelon, feta, and mint salad — is a creation that even a salad hater can love. The dish includes watermelon, which is made up of about 92% water and loaded with vitamin C and potassium. It’s the perfect sweet, hydrating food to combine with feta and mint leaves. Another touch of sweet comes from the honey in the dressing. I suggest using extra-virgin olive oil, if possible. A high-quality olive oil can make a huge difference in the taste. Arugula lettuce adds a unique, yet slightly bitter, taste. If you don’t like arugula you can substitute romaine lettuce.

The combination of sweet, bitter, and salty from the feta cheese in this nutritious salad presents an unforgettable flavor profile that keeps the taste buds curious. I’ve seen salad haters ask for seconds.

The second option — corn, tomato, and avocado salad — is another one of my summertime staples. I intentionally grill extra corn so that I can make this salad the next day. This dish has excellent sources of healthy fat that comes from the avocado and loads of antioxidants and flavor from the fresh herbs.

Often, people will use their avocados in the summer to make guacamole; however, guacamole, typically paired with tortilla chips, is too easy to consume in high volumes and with more fat, albeit healthy fat, and calories than you should consume in one sitting. This salad has just the right amount of avocado for a welcome, hearty taste. And just like the first salad, this dish is very easy to make. The lime juice and cilantro combination also adds some tanginess. Parsley is a good substitute for cilantro. For those who are monitoring their carbohydrate intake remember that corn is a starchy vegetable; therefore, this should be a side rather than a main dish.

I also suggest three simple and delicious ways to hydrate during the summer: The first beverage combines a lemon wedge, mint and a few cucumber slices in a glass of water or seltzer. The mint refreshes and the cucumber and lemon are excellent for hydration and treating dry mouth. The second drink combines a few basil leaves, a few strawberries and lime juice for some memorable sweet and tangy sips. Go ahead and substitute the strawberries with your preferred fruit. If you muddle the fruit you will release more of the flavor and increase the sweetness. The third suggestion is to simply slice up a large watermelon! Your fluid requirements can also come by way of fruit and vegetable consumption and melons come with a bonus of fiber, nutrients and are comprised of over 90% water.

Remember: As we enjoy the summer with family and friends, it’s still important to practice social distancing and maintain hygiene by washing hands frequently, especially when handling food and before eating. With creative salads, distinctive summer sips, and these precautions followed, you and your loved ones are on your way to a safe, healthy summer season.
