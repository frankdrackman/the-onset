Tom Kha Gai: A warm embrace from Thai cuisine

By Cewin Chao, MS, MBA Director, Clinical Nutrition Services · 2 min read · Tom Kha Gai: Un abrazo de la cocina Tailandesa

In short

Venturing beyond traditional chicken soup, the Thai version known as Tom Kha Gai marries the familiar with alluring flavors. Exploring chicken soup recipes from various cultures offers a way to experiment with and appreciate new ingredients.

Dive into a bowl of comfort bursting with nutrients and vibrant flavors.

The nutritional benefits of Tom Kha Gai are as varied as its ingredients. Chicken, the soup's traditional protein, is an excellent source of lean protein, crucial for muscle repair and overall body health. Besides adding a creamy texture and rich flavor, coconut milk deepens the soup's complexity and introduces a wealth of vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants, supporting heart health and immunity. Mushrooms contribute valuable fiber and B vitamins. With their distinctive flavors, lemongrass and galangal root have anti-inflammatory properties, aiding digestion and boosting immunity. Finally, fresh cilantro is a potent antioxidant that can help protect your cells from damage, and assists in cleansing your body of toxins to support the smooth functioning of your gut.

Preparing Tom Kha Gai is straightforward yet yields complex flavors. The process begins with simmering chicken in a savory broth and adding coconut milk for richness and depth of aromatics. The soup is then infused with the flavors of lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves, creating a base that's both tangy and savory. Mushrooms are added for their earthiness, and the dish is finished with a splash of lemon juice and a sprinkle of fresh cilantro for a vibrant, nourishing meal. For a plant-based version, use vegetable broth and replace the chicken with crispy tofu.

What makes this recipe special is its adaptability to include various vegetables. I enjoy adding spinach or kale towards the end of cooking, introducing extra vitamins, minerals, and fiber for a healthful boost. If you don't have these on hand, swiss chard, bok choy, collard greens, or beet greens are also excellent leafy greens. For an extra crunch you can add bell peppers, carrots, snow peas, baby corn, or bamboo shoots.

Tom Kha Gai exemplifies the pleasure of cooking with health in mind, showcasing that nutritious meals can be effortlessly satisfying and tailored to personal tastes and dietary needs.

Prepare this delicious and different traditional Thai recipe. Tom Kha Gai Chicken Soup is a combination of flavors you will love.

16 oz boneless chicken thigh, skinless and cut into strips

1 inner stem of lemongrass or a tablespoon of lemon zest

0.5-inch piece of galangal or 0.5-inch piece of fresh ginger

2 cups of the leafy green vegetable of your choice: spinach or kale leaves, optional*

*The % Daily Value (DV) tells you how much a nutrient in a food serving contributes to a daily diet. 2,000 calories a day is used for general nutrition advice.
