Vegan Cassoulet: Rustic elegance with hearty plant delights

Reviewed by Jessica Shapiro, MS, CDCES. Nutrition Program Manager, Montefiore Einstein Advanced Care · 2 min read · Cassoulet Vegano. Receta vegetariana provincial, con un toque sofisticado

In short

From reducing our carbon footprint to boosting our health, incorporating vegetarian options into our dinner repertoire can be a game-changing decision. Today, I invite you to join me as we explore the protein-packed world of vegan ingredients, the transformative powers of tofu, and the elusive umami flavor.

  • Reviewed by Jessica Shapiro, MS, Montefiore Einstein

French flair with every sustainable bite.

Lentils, chickpeas, peas, and beans, are bursting with enough protein to rival traditional meat-based counterparts. Beans are a versatile and economical protein source, but also benefit from being utterly scrumptious. Take our Vegan Cassoulet, for example. We're calling for cooked lentils and cannellini beans, both superstars in the worlds of protein, fiber, folate, and iron. Don't have these on hand? Don't worry! Cooked chickpeas, black beans, red kidney beans, or navy beans make perfect substitutes.

When it comes to the champions' league of plant-based proteins, tofu is a clear front-runner. Made from condensed soy milk, this protein-rich powerhouse is exceptionally adaptable. With the right technique, tofu can elevate any dish from the ‘everyday’ to the ‘gourmet’. The key is to press the excess water out of your tofu before using it. This quick and easy process improves the texture and it also enables the tofu to more effectively soak up marinades to enhance the flavor. For this recipe, we pan-fry the tofu until it's golden brown, achieving a delightful crunch on the outside while preserving a soft, creamy interior.

Often characterized as a savory or meaty taste, umami lends depth and richness to meatless dishes.

In the flavor universe, we're all familiar with sweet, sour, bitter, and salty. But there's a fifth, and lesser known flavor, Umami. Often characterized as a savory or meaty taste, umami lends depth and richness to meatless dishes. Numerous plant-based ingredients are naturally rich in umami. In our recipe, we're using tomatoes and mushrooms, both famous for their high umami content, to impart a savory depth that makes our Vegan Cassoulet utterly irresistible.
