Nutrition-filled oats are more than an ingredient for breakfast

By Jessica Shapiro, MS, CDCES, for Montefiore · 2 min read

In short

When you think of eating oats what typically comes to mind is oatmeal for breakfast However, oats can be prepared in a variety of ways, with different ingredients, and can be consumed at any time of the day, providing you have the right recipe. We have you covered! Try out this savory oat dish; a risotto infused with mushrooms, which can be prepared to accompany fish or chicken.

Oats are an edible grain that is native to Europe, Asia, and northwest Africa. They are cultivated in the United States for both human and animal consumption. As the third most important crop grain in the U.S., oats are a staple ingredient in cereal and are considered a “healthy food” due to their nutritional value. Oats have soluble fiber, which along with its cholesterol-lowering properties, is attributed to reducing the risk of heart disease. Including high-fiber ingredients like oats into your diet will also aid in keeping your digestive system healthy and in keeping your bowel function regular. Further, the combination of fiber and protein that oats contain can help you in maintaining blood sugar levels, feeling full faster, and promoting a healthy weight.

If you’re not already familiar with savory oats, then this recipe is a great jumping-off point. The earthy mushrooms infuse a warm umami flavor. Mushrooms are an excellent source of zinc, a good source of potassium, and one of the only food sources of vitamin D. They infuse our bodies with these much-needed nutrients for bone health and also have an anti-inflammatory effect on the immune system.

For the new year, don’t limit the nutritional goodness of oats to just breakfast. Enjoy the healthy benefits of oats (and mushrooms) all day long. Master the preparation of this savory dish of Oat Risotto with Mushrooms because it’s likely to become a favorite for lunch or dinner!
