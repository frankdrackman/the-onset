You'll get wrapped up in these heart-healthy cabbage wraps

By Monica Kelly, MS, Healthy Nutrition · 1 min read · Te fascinarán estos wraps de repollo saludables para el corazón

In short

As the weather gets warmer, I find myself craving light, fresh meals I can whip up and meal prep in batches. These healthy wraps are fully plant-based, which makes them great for anyone trying to follow a more plant-centric diet as recommended by the American Heart Association. The black beans in this wrap are a fantastic source of protein, and promote gut health by feeding the good bacteria in our microbiome.

Ditch the tortillas to create a high-fiber meal rich in vitamins

Using cabbage as a wrap not only keeps the calorie count low, it provides a hefty dose of vitamin C and anthocyanins, a naturally occurring antioxidant. The bell pepper inside the wrap adds even more antioxidants, which can reduce the inflammation that contributes to so many chronic illnesses.

The brown rice in this recipe kicks the nutritional content up another notch, since it provides fiber, vitamins and minerals that white rice lacks. Its low glycemic index also prevents insulin spikes, making it an excellent choice for diabetics.

1 ½ cups cooked black beans, drained or 1 15-ounce can of beans, drained and rinsed

*The % Daily Value (DV) tells you how much a nutrient in a food serving contributes to a daily diet. 2,000 calories a day is used for general nutrition advice.
