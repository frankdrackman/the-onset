Breastfeeding is good for infants and boosts mother's health as well

By By: Talitha L. Bruney, M.D., Cancer Care · 2 min read · La lactancia materna es una elección personal: Descubre los beneficios para bebés y mamás

In short

One of the most important decisions a new mother will make is how she will feed her newborn. Many women know breastfeeding is healthy for babies, but they may not know exactly why, or may not have heard how beneficial it can be for their own health. August is National Breastfeeding Month, when the USA joins much of the world in promoting awareness and support for families making decisions about this important choice. Breastfeeding is not a one-size-fits-all situation; there is flexibility for every woman to tailor the experience to her needs and those of her baby.

The decision to breastfeed is strictly a personal matter, though friends and family may have strong opinions on the subject. More than 80% of newborns start out exclusively breastfeeding, which is highly recommended by experts for the first six months of life. However, that number falls by more than half after three months, when many mothers start supplementing with formula or switching completely. This decline happens for many reasons.

First of all, breastfeeding can be difficult for the first few weeks as the baby is establishing his or her eating and sleeping patterns. Mother is the only source of food for the newborn, whose stomach is tiny. The need to feed the baby frequently makes going out or even getting a few hours of sound sleep a challenge. Mothers who work or go to school may find themselves in situations that are not conducive to breastfeeding or pumping their milk in private. Formula is marketed as a substitute for breast milk, and in some cases it is a necessary solution. Though Formula will meet the baby’s nutritional requirements, it cannot provide the natural benefits discussed above. The ideal is to breastfeed exclusively for six months, but each mother deserves to be supported whatever her circumstances may be and whatever choice she makes.

Most of all, remember that it gets easier, and many women appreciate the convenience in addition to the health and economic benefits. A mother who chooses to use formula will still provide her baby with all the required nutrients, and experience the closeness of feeding time, though without the natural benefits of breastfeeding. Again, it is a personal choice and one that each mother will want to consider carefully and discuss with her doctor in advance.

We are proud to be designated Baby-Friendly by Baby-Friendly USA, a global program sponsored by the World Health Organization (WHO) and the United Nations Children’s Fund (UNICEF). Montefiore offers prenatal education on breastfeeding, and our clinic staff are available to provide advice and reassurance on the phone, in person, and with apps. Our doctors, nurses, lactation specialists, and support staff are here to help, with respect for every mother, whatever choice she makes.
