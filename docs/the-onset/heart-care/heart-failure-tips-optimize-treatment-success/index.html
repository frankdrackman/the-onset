Heart failure tips to optimize treatment success

By Dr. Ulrich Jorde, and Marie Galvao, Montefiore · 4 min read

In short

Twenty years ago, a patient with advanced heart failure had only a ten percent chance of living for one year. Now that rate is 90 percent, thanks to greater understanding of the condition, new medications, and advances in surgical procedures and the use of innovative devices to assist the failing heart. More than 6 million Americans are living with heart failure, with another 550,000 new cases being diagnosed annually. Also called congestive heart failure, the condition is a progressive weakening of the heart that may come on so gradually that the early symptoms are overlooked. While there is no cure for heart failure, there are very effective treatments to slow or arrest its progression. The earlier the diagnosis, the sooner treatment can begin to improve your health and prolong your life. Even for people with advanced heart failure, there is hope and help.

Montefiore leads the fight against advanced heart failure

Heart failure used to be a deadly disease, but it doesn’t need to be any more. If you are short of breath, your legs are swollen, a family member has been diagnosed with heart failure, these are all reasons to be screened for heart failure so you can start the treatment prolongs life. Every Montefiore adult care clinic can screen for heart failure and its common causes, help you control high blood pressure, manage diabetes, support weight control, make heart-healthy lifestyle adjustments, and slow the progression of the condition. In partnership with your provider - remember, it takes two to tango - heart failure and the diseases that cause it can be successfully managed. If your condition does not improve despite taking medication and making lifestyle changes, request a referral to a heart failure specialist.

For people with advanced heart failure, The Montefiore Einstein Center for Heart and Vascular Care is recognized as one of the best in the nation by U.S. News & World Report. The Center is home to Montefiore’s Center for Advanced Cardiac Therapy, where we’re leading the fight against advanced heart failure using an integrated team approach to provide treatment that is individualized for each patient. The Montefiore Advanced Heart Failure Program is one of only 15 in the entire country participating in the American College of Cardiology’s Patient Navigator program which supports a team of caregivers assisting each patient during and after hospital stays to ease recovery and reduce the risk of readmission, enhancing long-term wellness. Medical and surgical therapies, including implanted devices to assist heart function, are available along with access to exclusive studies of promising new therapies for advanced heart disease. Our world-class surgeons offer a full spectrum of surgical therapies for advanced heart failure, including ventricular assist devices and heart transplant.
