In short

Hashimoto’s disease is a mysterious condition—know what to look for. Don’t miss S2 of The Balance with Dr. Philip O. Ozuah, President & CEO, Montefiore Einstein. Getting to the root cause when symptoms of a thyroid disorder are often misinterpreted. Dr. Ozuah’s guests are Montefiore Einstein endocrinologist, Dr. Vafa Tabatabaie, and Tanya Dios, who is dancing again because she was persistent.
