The Balance, with Dr. Philip Ozuah

The Balance is Montefiore Einstein’s video series, hosted by Dr. Philip Ozuah, President and Chief Executive Officer, and featured on the YES Network. Each episode pairs Dr. Ozuah with a Montefiore Einstein clinician and a real patient — the person who lived it explains what happened, and the clinician explains the medicine. Two seasons, thirteen episodes, five to eight minutes each, every one of them transcribed in full on this site.

Common questions about the series

How long is each episode of The Balance?

Between four and eight minutes. The shortest is A Little Does A Lot at 4:41 and the longest is Never Too Young at 7:54. Every episode is transcribed in full on this site, so you can read one in less time than it takes to watch it.

How many episodes are there, and is there a new season coming?

Thirteen: six in Season 1, released in May 2022, and seven in Season 2, released in June 2023. A season is released all at once rather than weekly, so the series arrives in a burst and then goes quiet between seasons.

Where can I watch The Balance?

On YouTube, embedded on each episode page here, and on the YES Network where the series is featured. The summary, the chaptered transcript and the guest clinician’s details live on montefioreeinstein.org so you can read, search and cite them without leaving.
