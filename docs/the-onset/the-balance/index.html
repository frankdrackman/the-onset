The Balance, with Dr. Philip Ozuah

The Balance is Montefiore Einstein’s video series, hosted by Dr. Philip Ozuah, President and Chief Executive Officer. Each episode pairs Dr. Ozuah with a Montefiore Einstein clinician for an unhurried conversation about one thing people actually worry about — sleep, the heart, the gut, the appointment they have been putting off. Two seasons, thirteen episodes, every one of them transcribed on this site.

Common questions about the series

Who is The Balance for?

Anyone deciding whether something is worth a doctor’s appointment. The series is made for the health-curious public rather than for clinicians — no jargon, no assumed knowledge, and every episode stands alone.

How long is each episode?

Between twelve and seventeen minutes. Every episode is transcribed in full on this site, so you can read it instead of watching it.

Where do the episodes play?

On YouTube, embedded on each episode page here. The transcript, the summary and the questions live on montefioreeinstein.org so you can read, search and cite them without leaving.
